# Redistribution decisions

| Provider | Provider's own terms | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| UK Parliament | [Open Parliament Licence](https://www.parliament.uk/site-information/copyright-parliament/open-parliament-licence/) | Short Hansard quotations on the page with speaker/date/source links; no full contributions in this package. Attribution: Contains Parliamentary information licensed under the Open Parliament Licence v3.0. |
| SiBol / Sketch Engine | [Corpus description](https://www.sketchengine.eu/sibol-corpus/), [terms of use](https://www.sketchengine.eu/terms-of-use/) | No general redistribution licence for newspaper concordances was established. Withhold all corpus text and raw API responses. Publish our annotations, aggregates, query parameters, source dates, locators and context hashes only. Reader needs their own licensed access to inspect contexts. Do not infer rights to newspaper text from rights to our annotations. |
| Govinfo | [Public domain and copyright policy](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies) | Government material may be public domain, but reprinted newspaper material retains its own rights. The 1966 and 1999 Record sources contain reprints: link and paraphrase only; withhold source PDFs and transcriptions. |
| Federal Reserve Board | Official speech and [government-work rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies) | Link and paraphrase the 2011 speech; no Federal Reserve text is redistributed. The attempted separate website-policy URL was unavailable. |
| ECB | [Copyright](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/disclaimer/html/index.en.html) | Link and paraphrase the 2023 interview; do not redistribute its transcript. |
| Google Books Ngram | [Provider FAQ: licensing and citation](https://books.google.com/ngrams/info), [download terms](https://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv3.html) | Publish the returned phrase-frequency data with attribution and the retained CC BY 3.0 terms used by this repository. No Books page text or political-sense annotation is implied. |
| GDELT / Quora | No usable result acquired | No text published; record failed access only. |
