# Method: wealth control

The source observations in `data/` are transcribed facts, not fitted model parameters. The original analysis selected four historical transitions and retained each denominator: factory motor horsepower, population, net fund assets and spot-FX turnover. They are deliberately not added together or treated as the same economic quantity. The earliest observation of each series starts its displayed elapsed-year clock.

Judgement steps are selecting analogies, deciding whether their denominators concern adoption, automation or economic control, and deciding whether any analogy supports an aggregate wealth claim. The model scenarios in the broader private exploration are not empirical estimates and are not inputs to this snack. No forecast is fitted here.

The payment-count and payment-value distributions describe the same three instruments in 2020 but have different denominators. The script checks each total within 0.015 percentage points to allow published rounding. It then compares each CSV exactly against the archived chart table.

The stock comparison is `ai_share_future × total_future_real > human_controlled_baseline_real`. Equality is not more. For a future real stock twice the baseline, 50% equals baseline; 60% exceeds it. For unchanged stock, 100% equals baseline. Turnover, asset ownership, transaction execution and delegated control require distinct attribution rules and cannot be substituted silently.

The result is an arithmetic and analogy check. A successful rerun does not prove the 2035 prediction or establish a uniquely valid definition of control. Primary-source transcription and semantic boundaries remain separately reviewable.
