Conventions and evidence

Period ratings and sources by convention

These ratings are editorial judgements based on partial reading. They describe how a convention fits a period and, where specified, a genre or region. They are not frequency counts and cannot date a source.

    The lowest rating among the conventions in a reading guides its rank. “No evidence found” ranks like “rare”. With no source period selected, each reading ranks by its best-rated period.

    Typing the signs

    Use ^m, ^c and ^xx for superscripts, _X for a bar over X, |X̅| for a framed numeral and (I) or CIↃ for the apostrophus. A j can stand for i and a u for v.
