All six markers have qualified source support. No marker represents an observed licence-issue date: the month numbers combine selected programme lengths with conditional eligibility. California expressly permits its training licence to be obtained during the first 180 days of residency. India's month 54 is the academic-phase/final-examination endpoint before internship; it must not imply that the MBBS degree has already been awarded.

## Verdict by jurisdiction

Months run from the start of the selected first post-school programme. “Qualified” means the rule and sequence are supported, subject to application, assessments and the stated modelling limits.

## Findings to incorporate before release

1. State visibly that registration markers show conditional eligibility on a selected route, rather than measured issue dates. Existing California stage notes already acknowledge administration is unmodelled; apply that distinction consistently to the page.
2. Correct the source excerpt attached to `ca-exam12`. The source page supports PTL Steps 1 and 2CK, but the shared displayed excerpt presently names Steps 1, 2CK and 3, which belongs to the later P&S licence. Split or narrow the source excerpt; the numeric stage itself is correct.
3. Retain India's degree-award distinction: month 54 means completion of the academic/final-examination phase and eligibility for provisional registration, not an already awarded MBBS degree. Retain the dated, bounded NExT finding rather than an unconditional claim that it has been cancelled or will never apply.

Opening paragraph: “graduated from an approved medical school”; “enrolled in a Board-approved California residency program”. Full substantive text read. Examinations are an additional condition in the same paragraph.
- **CA2 — Medical Board of California, [Examination Requirement](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/Physicians-and-Surgeons-License/Examinations.aspx)**. Undated live guidance. USMLE section: “For a Postgraduate Training License (PTL), you must pass Steps 1 and 2CK of the USMLE examination.” Full substantive text read; P&S separately requires all three steps.
- **CA3 — Medical Board of California, [Physicians and Surgeons](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/default.aspx)**. Undated live guidance. Residency licensing paragraph: “within 180 days from their program start date”. Full substantive licensing text read. This prevents interpreting residency-start month 96 as the actual compulsory PTL issue date.
- **CA4 — AAMC, [What To Consider as You Decide on a Career in Medicine](https://students-residents.aamc.org/choosing-medical-career/what-consider-you-decide-career-medicine)**. Undated live guidance. Education section: “four years of college (undergraduate school), four years of medical school”. Full substantive page read through the web reader, including the “typically” qualification. This supports the conventional route, not the shortest legally permissible route.

## Reading limits and method

- Compared the canonical `routes.md`, generated `stages.csv` first-registration records, and the timeline's milestone explanation with the independently retrieved sources above.
- Tested the ordering of academic training, embedded clinical work, examinations, first registration and initial postgraduate training. No added pre-registration year or omitted compulsory pre-registration postgraduate year was found in the six checked cases. India's distinction between academic completion and degree award needs explicit wording.
- Programme duration is not the same as a statutory minimum; California's conventional eight years, England's normal five years, Bavaria's standard course and NTU's selected programme must retain those labels.
- Administrative processing, examination scheduling, graduation dates, admission waits and individual delays were not measured. Registration does not guarantee a training appointment. No individual licence records or cohort completion distributions were checked.
- This bounded spot check does not verify every later GP/specialty endpoint, service obligation, alternative admission route, international graduate route or educational outcome. No inference about competence follows from these durations.
- The earlier Indian government-hospital SOP timed out in this independent check. The national original documents above provide the reviewed basis instead; no unread SOP passage is presented as independently verified.
