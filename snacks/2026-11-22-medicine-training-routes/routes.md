# Coding method

These are selected full-time domestic routes, not a census of possible routes or observed practitioner completion times. Each stage is separately coded in months. Zero-duration events mark a prerequisite or eligibility milestone; they do not assert that examinations and registration processing take no time. Stage notes identify embedded requirements and overlaps. First registration may permit supervised practice only. A general medical licence, completion of initial clinical training, GP specialty qualification and authority to run a clinic are distinct endpoints.

Sources are the regulators' own rules wherever available, with programme bodies used for an explicitly selected conventional degree or specialist example. Every stage resolves to a dated source register entry and short source passage; CSV export expands those references. Reading-depth limitations remain part of the evidence. Original source documents are retained only in ignored scratch, subject to their terms. No reader should use this comparative preview to determine an individual's eligibility for medical registration.
