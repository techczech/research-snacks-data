## Remaining limits and source disagreements

No human epigraphist validation; no agent run; no new model test. Several sources were inspected only as abstracts, excerpts or indexed passages. Corpus totals for Khitan/Meroitic and proposed ICIT/ET²/Urcid replacements remain unaudited. Exact Bushell 1896/37-character attribution, Khitan 1975 group formation, literal completeness of the Langjun translation and alternate Linear A document convention remain unverified.

Sources do not support replacing the 2023 Issyk-Kushan unknown-language identification with a settled claim; the 2026 paper advances Bactrian readings. They also qualify simple date claims: Norris has 1853/1854/1855 records; Mnamon distinguishes Iberian 1922 publication and 1925 sign-reading steps. The Zaytsev source corrects “Nova N 176” to “Nova H 176”; its first-page footnote estimates the manuscript’s signs from page/line counts rather than providing a counted 15,000-token corpus.

Reproduction verifies arithmetic from saved judgements, not truth of those judgements or acceptance of any proposal. No release tag created for this round.
