# Source terms and sharing decisions

Read date: 19 November 2026. This is a limited set of attributed numerical facts and calculations, not a mirror of source publications. A public PDF does not confer an open licence, including when a government archive holds a third-party publication. No source PDF, original chart, source prose or full restricted dataset is included.

Ship attributed numerical observations and locators. No logos, photos or third-party images. Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (Annual Energy Review 2011, September 2012); U.S. Energy Information Administration (Nuclear Plant Cancellations: Causes, Costs, and Consequences, April 1983); U.S. Energy Information Administration (Nuclear Power Plant Construction Activity 1986, 1987). |
