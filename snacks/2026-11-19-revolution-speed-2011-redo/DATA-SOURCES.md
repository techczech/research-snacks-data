# Sources and redistribution decisions

## NAVCO 1.3

Provider citation, copied verbatim from [Harvard Dataverse](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/ON9XND):

> Chenoweth, Erica; Christopher Wiley Shay, 2020, "List of Campaigns in NAVCO 1.3", https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/ON9XND, Harvard Dataverse, V1, UNF:6:GD3vspZ7XK7H7KFdiKFICw== [fileUNF]

Provider licence: **CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication**, [licence text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This was verified in the dataset's [metadata export](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/datasets/export?exporter=dataverse_json&persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/ON9XND), `datasetVersion.license`, and the provider's indexed dataset page on 19 November 2026. This public, preliminary release contains 622 rows and sixteen fields. It is not an invented version of NAVCO 2.x or 3.0.

The original tab-separated campaign list is redistributed as `data/navco-1.3.tab`. Exact endpoint: [original data file](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/:persistentId?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/ON9XND/XRA6WM). The [codebook](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/:persistentId?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/ON9XND/PTMCCV) was read for the fields and outcome rules; it is linked, not bundled. CC0 applies to the provider data; our broader repository licence does not replace it. The code preserves duplicate NAVCO IDs because they label separate rows.

## Historical chronologies and official records

The page's [complete linked references](https://researchsnacks.semanticmachines.fyi/2026-11-19-revolution-speed-2011-redo/#references) identify the providers. `data/cases.csv` and `data/boundaries.csv` cite the source for each judgement. No blanket permission to redistribute their websites, photographs, full tables or PDFs is inferred. We distribute only individually transcribed event dates, short descriptions in our own wording and calculations. The private archive is withheld. These original annotations are CC BY 4.0 to the extent that rights subsist; underlying facts are not claimed as our exclusive property.

Additional locators: ÚSTR chronology's 17/24/29 November and 10/29 December headings; CRS Egypt sections 25 January and 11 February; French National Assembly's February 1848 account; Winship's 20 March 1917 dispatch, with explicit calendar conversion; Iranica's 1978 and 1979 chronology; Philippines Proclamation 59; LeMO 4 September and 18 October entries; George Mason University's Romania exhibition; RFE/RL Newsline 28 September 2000 and CANVAS printed p.179; IFES's Georgia election note; State Department's Tunisia background note; Libya commission A/HRC/19/68; CRS Yemen printed pp.1–2 (Figure 1 is an image, visually inspected); UN Sudan communication UA SDN 1/2019 and Security Council Report June 2019; OHCHR's Sri Lanka protest statement and Parliament's 16 July proceedings; UN Bangladesh communication AL BGD 6/2024 and fact-finding report paragraphs 52–54.

The article sources remain the property of their providers. Some download attempts failed; working primary PDFs and institutional mirrors were used where available. No complete source publication is redistributed. This is a limited factual extraction, not a licence for republishing the underlying documents.

## Tahrir protester survey

[Tufekci and Wilson (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1460-2466.2012.01629.x), *Social Media and the Decision to Participate in Political Protest: Observations From Tahrir Square*, Journal of Communication 62(2), 363–379. [Accepted manuscript read](https://www.theengineroom.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/jcom_1629_review.pdf), pp.5 and 8. The manuscript states International Communication Association copyright. No reuse licence for respondent data was established; no respondent records, manuscript or source graphic is shipped. Five brief published factual observations are attributed in `data/egypt-survey-inputs.csv`; no new survey analysis is claimed. These figures do not sum to 100 because lesser first-information sources are omitted from the small table.

## Sources inspected without a dataset merge

[Archigos](https://rochester.edu/college/faculty/hgoemans/data.htm), [Powell–Thyne coups](https://jonathanmpowell.com/coups/) and Mass Mobilization Project were considered. No copies of these datasets are distributed, no inferred redistribution licence is granted and no merge is claimed. No ITU/World Bank internet-use values are included. The NAVCO list supplies the campaign denominator; the dated historical cases remain separate judgements.
