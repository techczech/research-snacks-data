## Rejected candidate

The current OpenStax Psychology 2e PDF says ©2026 Rice University and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. A search snippet referring to an older CC BY 4.0 version did not establish rights in this download. No excerpt or PDF from it is distributed. The narrower Python source avoided adding non-commercial and share-alike restrictions to another part of this public package.
